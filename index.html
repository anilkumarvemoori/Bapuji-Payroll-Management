Reports

Settings

Allowance & deduction types, statutory defaults, and the connection to your Google Sheet.

Login & Users

Manage who can sign in to this app. Anyone in this list can log in with their username and password.

Passwords are checked by the Google Sheet and stored there only as scrambled (hashed) values — nobody, including admins, can read them back. A forgotten password can be reset here. Keep edit access to the Sheet itself limited to people you trust.

Institutions

Manage the list of institutions (e.g. Bapuji, Balaji). These appear as a dropdown when adding or editing staff, and as a filter across Staff Management, Run Payroll, and Reports.

Designations

Manage the list of job titles/roles (e.g. English Teacher, Accountant). These appear as a dropdown when adding or editing staff, and as a filter in Staff Management.

Branches

Manage the list of branches. These appear as a dropdown when adding or editing staff, and as a filter in Staff Management.

Allowance & Deduction Types

These appear as editable columns in Run Payroll. Add as many as you need — TA, DA, HRA, uniform deduction, loan recovery, etc.

Statutory Defaults

Default PF and ESI percentages applied to new staff (calculated on base salary). Override per staff on their record.

Connection

This app is pre-configured to fetch permanently from your Google Sheet — every visit, on every device, reads live data. Only change the URL below if you need to point it at a different Sheet (e.g. testing).
